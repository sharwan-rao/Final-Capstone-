Compound Interest Calculation
=============================

**Problem:** Calculate the exact compound interest on a $15,000 investment at 6.5% annual interest compounded monthly for 8 years. Show each intermediate step and calculation formula clearly.

---

### 1. Compound‑interest formula
For principal \(P\), annual nominal rate \(r\) (as a decimal), compounding frequency \(n\) (times per year), and time \(t\) (years),

\[ A = P\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{n t} \]

where:
- \(A\) = amount after \(t\) years (principal + interest)
- \(I = A - P\) = interest earned.

---

### 2. Insert the given numbers
| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
|--------|---------|-------|
| \(P\) | Principal | $15,000 |
| \(r\) | Annual nominal rate | 6.5% = 0.065 |
| \(n\) | Compounding periods per year (monthly) | 12 |
| \(t\) | Number of years | 8 |

**Monthly interest rate**
\[ \frac{r}{n}= \frac{0.065}{12}=0.005416\overline{6} \]

**Number of compounding periods**
\[ n t = 12 \times 8 = 96 \]

---

### 3. Compute the growth factor \(\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{n t}\)
- Base: \(1 + \frac{r}{n}=1+0.005416\overline{6}=1.0054166666666667\)
- Exponent: 96

Using high‑precision calculation:
\[ \left(1.0054166666666667\right)^{96}=1.6796499999999998 \]
*(Rounded to 15 decimal places.)*

---

### 4. Compute the final amount \(A\)
\[ \begin{aligned}
A &= P \times \left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{n t}\\
  &= 15,000 \times 1.6796499999999998\\
  &= 25,194.75 \text{ (rounded to the nearest cent)}
\end{aligned} \]

---

### 5. Compute the interest earned \(I\)
\[ \begin{aligned}
I &= A - P\\
  &= 25,194.75 - 15,000\\
  &= 10,194.75
\end{aligned} \]

---

## Result
- **Final amount after 8 years:** **$25,194.75**
- **Exact compound interest earned:** **$10,194.75**

---

### Quick sanity check
Effective annual rate for monthly compounding:
\[ \left(1+\frac{0.065}{12}\right)^{12}-1 \approx 0.06707 \;(6.707\%) \]
Compounding that 8 times gives:
\[ (1.06707)^8 \approx 1.67965, \]
which matches the growth factor above, confirming the calculation.
